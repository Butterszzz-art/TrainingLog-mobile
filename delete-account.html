Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can permanently delete your Pocket Coach account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need the app installed to do it.

Option 1: Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open Pocket Coach in the app, or sign in on the web at this site.
  2. Go to Settings → App → Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. Want a copy of your data first? Download it from Progress → Tools (CSV or full JSON backup) before you delete.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, email armanbahali@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Pocket Coach account" and your username. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address so nobody else can delete your account. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

Cancel your subscription first

  • Bought on the web or Android (Stripe): deleting your account cancels it automatically.
  • Bought on iPhone or iPad (App Store): deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Apple will keep charging you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details, username and email address
  • Everything you've logged: workouts, sets, templates, programs, bodyweight and measurements, nutrition, cardio, sleep, readiness and check-ins, mobility and rehab notes, goals and progress photos
  • Your profile and photo, friendships, group memberships, posts, shared programs, leaderboard entries and notifications
  • Coach connections, including your coach's copy of your client record and the notes they sent you, and any coaching intake form you sent with the same email address

What we keep, and for how long

  • Billing records (plan, dates and amounts, held by Stripe or Apple) for as long as tax and accounting law requires — usually 7 years.
  • Backups that expire on a rolling basis, within 30 days.
  • Data sent to our AI provider (Anthropic), if you used AI features, which it deletes within 30 days.
  • Reports you made about other users, or that were made about you, where we need them to keep the community safe or meet a legal obligation.

Data stored on your device (check-ins, drafts, settings and progress photos) is removed from the device you delete your account on. Logging out alone keeps it, so entries that have not synced are not lost. On other devices, delete the app to remove it.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
