Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what Pocket Coach ("we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Pocket Coach is the data controller for your personal data. Contact: armanbahali@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: username, email address, and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us in plain text.
  • Profile information you choose to add: display name, bio, and profile photo.
  • Fitness and health data you log: workouts, exercises and weights lifted, bodyweight and body measurements, nutrition and macros, sleep logs, readiness and check-in scores, cardio, injuries or rehab notes, goals, and progress photos.
  • Activity data from a service you connect: if you connect an activity tracker in Settings → Activity Sync, we receive data such as steps and activity from that service. You can disconnect at any time.
  • Community and coaching data: groups you join, posts and programs you share, leaderboard entries, friend and coach connections, users you block, and reports you submit about other users.
  • Subscription information: your plan and subscription status. On iPhone and iPad, purchases are made through Apple's App Store: we receive the transaction details Apple provides (product, dates, and transaction ID), never your payment details. On the web and Android, card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and handled by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number.
  • App preferences: units, theme, training mode, and similar settings.

We don't use advertising trackers, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data.

Coaching intake form (before you have an account)

If you fill in the personal-coaching intake form, we collect what you enter there: name, email, age, height, weight and body-fat percentage, current strength, goals and motivation, training availability and job, injuries and exercises to avoid, diet and food preferences, use of performance-enhancing substances, activity, stress, sleep and caffeine, available equipment, supplements, wrist and ankle measurements, and optionally a hand photo and your current training program. From these we calculate starting calorie, macro and training-volume estimates.

  • Why and legal basis: to assess your request and prepare a coaching plan. Because much of this is health data, we only store it with your explicit consent (UK/EU GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), given by ticking the consent box on the form. The form can't be sent without it.
  • Who sees it: only your coach, through the coach dashboard. It is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage for any photo or file you upload with the form).
  • Notifications: your coach gets a short email via Web3Forms with only your name, email and that a new intake arrived — not your answers. If you chose a coaching package, the payment instructions are emailed to you via EmailJS (name, email and package only).
  • Copies: after sending, your browser downloads a copy of your answers for you, and you can choose to email that file to your coach yourself.
  • How long: if you become a client, your intake becomes part of your coaching record. If you don't, we delete your intake within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Deleting a Pocket Coach account also deletes intake submissions made with the same email address.
  • Withdrawing consent: email armanbahali@gmail.com and we will delete your intake. This doesn't affect processing that happened before.

3. AI Features

Pocket Coach's AI features (for example the AI coach, weekly briefs and reviews, program generation and program import, macro and sleep insights, plateau checks, rehab plans, and coach message drafts) are powered by AI models that we reach through OpenRouter, a service that forwards each request to an AI model provider. We currently use free models run by Google, NVIDIA, Alibaba (Qwen) and Nex AGI; which of them answers can change from one request to the next.

  • What is sent: the data needed for the feature you use — such as your recent workouts, exercises and weights, bodyweight, nutrition, sleep and readiness scores, goals, any message you type, and any file you import as a training program. We don't send your password or payment details.
  • Consent first: the app asks your permission before sending any data to OpenRouter and its model providers. If you decline, AI features stay off and the rest of the app works normally. You can change this at any time in Settings → App → Privacy & Legal.
  • How it is handled: OpenRouter passes the data to the model provider, which uses it to generate a response. OpenRouter and the providers handle it under their own terms and privacy policies (see OpenRouter's privacy policy). Providers of free models may store requests and may use them to improve their models. Only use AI features with information you are comfortable sharing on these terms, and don't type in details you don't need to, such as medical history.

5. International Transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the UK or EEA we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK/EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. AI requests sent through OpenRouter may be processed in the United States and in other countries where the chosen model provider runs its servers.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our systems straight away, except billing records we must keep for tax and accounting law, backups that expire on a rolling basis, and reports about other users that we need to keep the community safe. Deleting your account also removes your coach's copy of your coaching data. Some data (such as preferences, check-ins, draft logs and progress photos) is also stored on your device: deleting your account removes it from the device you delete it on. Logging out keeps it on the device so you don't lose entries that haven't synced; delete the app to remove it from other devices.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Export: download all your data at any time from Progress → Tools (CSV or full JSON backup).
  • Delete: permanently delete your account and data from Settings → App → Danger Zone — no need to contact us. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your account. This also cancels a subscription bought on the web or Android; an App Store subscription must be cancelled with Apple.
  • AI consent: turn AI data sharing on or off in Settings → App → Privacy & Legal.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or port your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing before you withdrew it). Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl); you can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live, or in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), stored with providers that encrypt data at rest, and access to it is limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Pocket Coach is not intended for anyone under 16. We ask you to confirm you are 16 or older when you sign up, and we don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post changes here with a new date, and tell you in the app if a change is significant.
