Common questions

Can I use Pocket Coach as just an athlete, for free?
Yes. Workout logging, macros, tracking, programs, check-ins, progress and community are all free, with no card needed.
Is there a limit on how many clients I can manage on Coach?
No. Coach is a flat monthly or annual fee however many clients you coach.
What AI features does Pro include?
An AI coach with daily briefs and weekly reviews, a program generator and plateau detector, macro, sleep and check-in insights, and rehab plans. They run on open AI models through Ollama and OpenRouter and only run after you agree to share the data they need. AI output can be wrong — always use your own judgement.
Can I try a paid plan before paying?
Yes — Pro and Coach both start with a 7-day free trial. Cancel any time during the trial and you won't be charged.
How do I cancel?
On the web or Android: Progress → Tools → Subscription in the app. On iPhone or iPad: your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
